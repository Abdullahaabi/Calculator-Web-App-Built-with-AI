# App Roles

**R-1 — Prospective Borrower.** A Prospective Borrower is an individual considering a fixed-rate loan and comparing affordability before contacting a lender. They can enter loan terms, view estimated payments and totals, and inspect a month-by-month repayment schedule. They must never receive a misleading numeric result for invalid inputs.

**R-2 — Budget Planner.** A Budget Planner is someone checking how a hypothetical loan fits a monthly household budget. They can adjust loan amount, interest and term to compare repayment estimates. They must never be shown stale results after entering invalid values.
